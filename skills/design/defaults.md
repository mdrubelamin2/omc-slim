# design: what gets called out

Opened from [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md), not read by default.

Two lists and a method. The first list does not decay and it gates. The second is dated, carries a real false-positive rate, and only ever advises.

Do not prescribe a replacement look. A prescribed look becomes the next default.

# What does not decay

Craft. These fail a build, and they are what people cite when they want to be right rather than fast.

**No real images.** Every section an icon in a tile above a heading above two lines. Most good sites run closer to half imagery.

**No coherence across sections.** Each block defensible alone, nothing shared. Glassy buttons here, heavy gradients there, a third shadow language below.

**Colour that does not mean what it says.** A yellow indicator against a status reading "Productive". Semantic colour applied by appearance rather than by state.

**Copy carrying no checkable fact.** After a paragraph, name one concrete thing it said. More than half failing means the words are decoration, and stripping the punctuation tells does not fix it.

**Invented numbers and unearned proof.** "Trusted by 10,000+ professionals" on a site with no traffic. Logos of companies with no relationship to the product.

# What is dated

**Calibrated 2026-09-05.** Past six months treat every item as a hypothesis to re-check.

Advisory only. Any one is a legitimate choice; all of them together is the fingerprint, and the finding is that none of them was decided.

**Colour and surface.** Violet-to-blue gradients. Gradient text on a heading. Dark background with neon or lime accents and a glow. Cream near `#F4F1EA` with a high-contrast serif and terracotta. Tinted near-black instead of black. A radial halo behind a hero. One uniform soft shadow under every card.

**Structure.** The default kit's symmetric centred hero, three feature cards, call to action. Three or six identical cards, each icon over heading over two lines. One border radius on every surface. A coloured border on one side of a rounded card. Nested cards. The hero metric template. Everything centred.

**Type.** A tracked-out all-caps eyebrow above a heading. One word in a headline recoloured. `01 / 02 / 03` on content that is not a sequence. An oversized italic serif hero. Monospace as costume. Meta joined with middle dots. An arrow appended to link text. A stock sans used because nothing else was considered, which is a different finding from the typeface itself.

**Filler.** Emoji as icons. The same thin-line icon set. Icons in coloured circles as decoration. Blobs and floating shapes. Procedural grain. Repeating stripes.

**Motion.** Fade-and-slide-up on every section. A hover lift on every card. Animation that does not describe what is happening, such as a card scaling while its contents stay put.

**Copy.** Em-dashes at a density no human sustains, which is a rate and not a presence. "It's not just X, it's Y" and "not only, but also". Marketing register where description was wanted: "empower your", "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless experience", "best-in-class", "transform your business". Aphoristic three-part cadence. Meta-commentary that addresses the reader. An error that apologises without naming what failed.

**Nothing here convicts alone.** Award-winning human work has been accused on every one of these, and the accusation is cheap and unfalsifiable. Report the pattern, name the count, never the verdict.

# Instead

**Put real images in.** Placeholders that are meant to be replaced, marked as such.

**Break the symmetry on purpose.** An asymmetric hero, one oversized real screenshot, specifics where the template wants a slogan.

**Name what you are ruling out.** The default reasserts itself through anything left unspecified.

**One surface, one dimension, per pass.** "Make this section less templated without changing the layout" beats "make it better".

**Spend the boldness in one place, then remove the weakest element.**

**Specificity is the whole counter-move.** A real number, a real moment, a real outcome. It cannot be faked by editing punctuation, and it is the one thing the median cannot supply.
